# NAVA CREATIONS — Phase 1.6 Brand Claims & Evidence Classification Register
## Rigorous Evidentiary Audit of Strategic Assertions, Cultural Tropes, and Marketing Statements

> **Document Type:** Brand Claims & Legal/Commercial Evidentiary Audit  
> **Brand Under Review:** NAVA CREATIONS (Bengaluru, Karnataka)  
> **Objective:** Systematically audit every strategic statement, separate verified facts from hypotheses, and eliminate unverified marketing claims to protect consumer trust and legal compliance.

## 3. Mandatory Copywriting Sanity Rules for Future Storefront Build

When Phase 2 and Phase 3 copywriting commences, the engineering and content team must enforce the following **Four Storefront Copy Rules**:

1. **Rule of Origin:** Use *"Thoughtfully hand-finished in Bengaluru, Karnataka"*. Never use *"Crafted in our Bengaluru atelier"*.
2. **Rule of Craft:** Use *"Handcrafted embellishments and delicate detailing"*. Never claim proprietary metal casting or exclusive heritage artisan guilds.
3. **Rule of Materials:** Describe materials accurately (*"Silk thread, Kundan stones, acrylic and brass core bases"*). Never use the word *"Solid Gold"* or *"Gemstones"* without explicit mineral verification.
4. **Rule of Sizing:** State clearly: *"Available in core sizes 2.4, 2.6, and 2.8. Custom sizes available on request with 7-day preparation"*.
