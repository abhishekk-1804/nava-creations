## 1. Introduction & Brand Philosophy

NAVA CREATIONS is an independent accessories and jewellery brand based in Bengaluru, Karnataka. These Brand Guidelines establish the non-negotiable rules governing the visual, typographic, and narrative expression of the brand across all physical and digital touchpoints.

### The Brand Golden Rule
> **"Elegance through Restraint, Authenticity through Transparency."**  
> We never rely on artificial scarcity, screaming discount badges, or deceptive claims. Luxury is communicated through generous whitespace, tactile craft, honest photography, and flawless customer care.

### 2.1 Clear Space Requirements
To maintain visual breathing room and protect brand integrity, all lockups must be surrounded by a mandatory **Clear Space** equal to the height of the letter **'N'** in the primary wordmark:

- On all four sides of the logo, no typography, illustration, photography edge, or border line may intrude into this exclusion zone.
- For the horizontal lockup, the minimum clearance margin is **`0.5 × Logo Height`**.

```
    ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │                 Clear Space (X)              │
    │     ┌──────────────────────────────────┐     │
    │ (X) │  [ICON]   NAVA CREATIONS         │ (X) │
    │     └──────────────────────────────────┘     │
    │                 Clear Space (X)              │
    └──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
    Where X = Cap-height of 'N' in NAVA
```

### 2.2 Minimum Display Sizes
To prevent typographic crushing and loss of vector detail:

| Lockup Version | Digital Minimum | Physical Print Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| **Horizontal Lockup** | Width: `140px` (Height ~`35px`) | Width: `38 mm` |
| **Stacked Lockup** | Width: `90px` (Height ~`80px`) | Width: `25 mm` |
| **Secondary Circular Seal** | Diameter: `60px` | Diameter: `18 mm` |
| **Standalone Icon / Favicon** | `32 × 32px` (`favicon.svg`) | `10 × 10 mm` |

## 4. Typography Hierarchy & Guidelines

```
DISPLAY XL (56px / 36px Mobile) ── Cormorant Garamond 500 Medium
"Handcrafted for the moments that stay."

HEADING 1 (36px / 26px Mobile) ── Cormorant Garamond 600 SemiBold
"Padmavati Heritage Kundan Chuda Set"

HEADING 2 (28px / 22px Mobile) ── Cormorant Garamond 500 Medium
"Curated Bridal Wristwear & Kundan Adornments"

SUB-HEADING / H3 (22px / 19px Mobile) ── Cormorant Garamond 600
"Bespoke Silk Threading & Sizing Precision"

LEAD PARAGRAPH (18px) ── DM Sans 400 Regular
"Every Kundan stone is hand-set into custom gold-toned lac by Bengaluru artisans."

BODY TEXT (16px / 15px Mobile) ── DM Sans 400 Regular
"Our bangles are measured by exact inner diameter in millimeters to ensure a fit that neither slips during celebrations nor pinches delicate skin."

OVERLINE / CATEGORY (11px, Tracking 0.18em) ── DM Sans 600 SemiBold Uppercase
BRIDAL CHUDA COLLECTION • BENGALURU ATELIER

PRICE DISPLAY (20px / 26px PDP) ── DM Mono 500 Medium Tabular
₹2,899.00
```

### 4.1 Strict Typographic Rules:
1. **Never substitute system serif fonts (Times New Roman) for Cormorant Garamond.**
2. **Never artificially stretch, compress, or italicize Cormorant Garamond.**
3. **Never render text smaller than 12px (0.75rem).** 12px is the strictly enforced accessible floor for captions, legal text, and tax disclosures.
4. **All numerical currency values must be set in `DM Mono`** to prevent tabular misalignment across checkout and invoice tables.

## 5. Unacceptable Logo Usages (Violations)

```
❌ IMPROPER LOGO USAGE VIOLATIONS:
1. DO NOT stretch, skew, or distort the aspect ratio of the logo.
2. DO NOT alter the relative scale between the icon and the wordmark.
3. DO NOT change the brand colors (e.g., rendering the logo in bright red or blue).
4. DO NOT apply drop shadows, 3D extrusions, or bevel filters to the vector logo.
5. DO NOT place the logo on busy, high-contrast, or unapproved photographic backgrounds.
6. DO NOT use Antique Gold (#C5A059) as body text beneath the logo.
7. DO NOT enclose the horizontal logo within an arbitrary non-brand container box.
8. DO NOT type the brand name as "Nava's Creations" or "NAVA CREATION" (singular).
```

## 6. Tone of Voice & Linguistic Register

### 6.1 Voice Pillars:
- **Poised & Dignified:** Speak with calm authority. Avoid exclamation mark overload (`!!!`) and aggressive sales language.
- **Culturally Attuned:** Celebrate Indian cultural heritage with authenticity. Use traditional terms like *Muhurtham*, *Kundan*, *Chuda*, *Shringara*, and *Karigari* with dignity.
- **Clear & Transparent:** When discussing materials, state base metals and lac settings honestly. Never claim solid precious gold or natural gemstones unless certified.

### 6.2 Customer Communication Example:
```
❌ BAD (Bazaar Cliché):
"HURRY LADIES! CRAZY 70% OFF SALE ON BRIDAL BANGLES! ORDER NOW ON WHATSAPP OR REGRET FOREVER!!!"

✅ GOOD (NAVA Standard):
"Crafted for your most cherished celebrations. Explore our curated Bridal Chuda edit, tailored to your exact wrist diameter in our Bengaluru atelier. Complimentary pan-India delivery on orders above ₹1,999."
```
