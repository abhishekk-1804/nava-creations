## 1. Commercial Rationale: Why Assumptions Are Fatal to E-Commerce

Building an e-commerce platform upon assumed pricing and unit costs creates severe commercial failure modes:
1. **The Packaging Margin Trap:** Designing a ₹140 rigid keepsake box when the business sells ₹400 bangles erodes 35% of revenue on packaging alone.
2. **The Checkout Friction Trap:** Requiring instant online prepaid payment if 70% of historical Instagram buyers expect cash-on-delivery (COD) or WhatsApp consultation drops conversion by 60%.
3. **The Sizing Return Trap:** Promising custom millimeter sizes without knowing inventory constraints causes high return rates and customer frustration.

To eliminate these risks, the business owner must supply ground-truth operational facts across the nine categories below.
