## Direction 1 — "Meri Baari" (Botanical Ornamental Wordmark)
*Elegant, Traditional Indian Motifs, Refined*

---

### Concept
A wordmark where the letterforms of **NAVA CREATIONS** are anchored by a hand-drawn botanical element drawn from Indian decorative tradition — specifically a stylised **bel patra** (vine leaf) or **parijata flower** rendered in a single continuous line. The mark evokes hand-embroidered borders, traditional meenakari floral work, and 19th-century Indian printmaking — but executed with editorial restraint, not folk-craft busyness.

The botanical element serves as a **brand mark** that can stand alone as the icon, with the wordmark flanking or sitting beneath it.

### Design Rationale
- Indian botanical motifs are universally legible as *Indian* without being culturally specific to one region or religion — accessible across Hindu, Muslim, and secular contexts
- Single-line illustration (not filled, not embossed) reads as handcraft — aligns with the maker narrative
- Paijata/bel leaf motif has established auspicious associations without religious exclusivity
- Avoids the "wedding invitation" trap by being minimal line art, not embellished ornament

### Typography Recommendation

| Role | Font | Weight | Source |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| **Primary wordmark** | [Cormorant Garamond](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cormorant+Garamond) | Light (300) or Regular | Google Fonts |
| **Sub-wordmark "CREATIONS"** | Cormorant Garamond | Light Italic | Google Fonts |
| **Fallback / UI body** | [DM Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans) | Regular 400 | Google Fonts |

**Typographic treatment**: "NAVA" in Cormorant Garamond at approximately 2.5× the size of "CREATIONS", set in wide letter-spacing (tracking: 0.15–0.25em on CREATIONS). "NAVA" is the dominant visual word; "CREATIONS" acts as a descriptor line.

### Symbol / Icon Concept
A single unbroken line drawing of a **five-petal parijata flower** where:
- The petals are formed by one continuous stroke that curves back on itself
- The stem line extends below the flower and curves into the first letter of NAVA, optionally connecting the icon to the wordmark
- Scale: at full size, the flower sits approximately 1.8× the cap-height of the N
- Style: 1.2pt stroke weight, no fill, no gradient, perfectly reproducible at any size
- The line weight matches the stroke of the letterforms in Cormorant Garamond so the mark reads as a unified system

### Wordmark Relationship
**Stacked lockup (primary):** Icon centred above, "NAVA" below the icon, "CREATIONS" in smaller type below that — three-tier vertical composition.
**Horizontal lockup (secondary):** Icon at left, "NAVA" immediately right at large scale, "CREATIONS" in a smaller tracking below the word NAVA — works for website header.
**Icon-only (social, favicon):** The parijata flower alone, works as monogram substitute.

### Colour Direction
| Colour | HEX | Use |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Primary deep plum | `#5C2449` | Icon + "NAVA" wordmark |
| Antique gold | `#C9A96E` | "CREATIONS" text, fine line decoration |
| Warm ivory | `#FAF6F0` | Background / reversed lockup |

### Light / Dark Application
- **On white (#FFFFFF)**: Plum wordmark + gold "CREATIONS" — high contrast, premium
- **On cream (#FAF6F0)**: Plum + gold — warmest rendering; use for packaging
- **On deep plum (#5C2449)**: Reversed — icon and wordmark in warm ivory `#FAF6F0`, "CREATIONS" in antique gold
- **On black**: Reserved for high-impact editorial; use ivory mark

### Favicon / Instagram Icon Adaptation
- **32px favicon**: The parijata flower only, in plum on cream; 5 petals with clean separation. No text.
- **180px Apple touch icon**: Flower on plum background in cream/ivory
- **1080×1080 Instagram profile icon**: The flower mark centred on a deep plum circle, no wordmark (text is too small to read as an avatar)
- **Instagram stories/reels watermark**: Horizontal lockup, bottom-left corner, cream on dark background

### Advantages
- Highest alignment with Indian cultural vocabulary without cultural specificity
- The botanical line element has genuine craft references (aligns with handmade story)
- Scales gracefully from packaging to digital
- Cormorant Garamond is free, beautiful, and genuinely premium-looking
- Icon can stand alone as an instant recognisable mark

### Trade-offs
- Floral marks are common in Indian accessories; differentiation depends heavily on execution quality
- Cormorant Garamond requires a skilled hand at small sizes; web rendering needs care
- Detailed line art icon may not engrave well on very small physical packaging

### Best Fit: Customer Segments
**Strongest resonance with**: Bridal customers, Festival shoppers, Gift buyers
**Secondary resonance**: Everyday buyers who value Indian heritage

## Direction 2 — "NC Monogram" (Contemporary Luxury Monogram)
*Modern, Minimal, International*

---

### Concept
A precision-drawn **interlocking monogram of N and C** — NAVA CREATIONS — constructed on a geometric grid. The letterforms are custom-modified from a modern serif (not an off-the-shelf ligature), with the C curving to embrace the vertical stroke of the N, suggesting completeness, encirclement, and the shape of a **bangle** in its negative space. The wordmark "NAVA CREATIONS" appears in a clean contemporary serif below the monogram, spaced with authority.

### Design Rationale
- Monogram marks are internationally associated with luxury (Cartier, Chanel, Louis Vuitton) — this positions NAVA as aspirationally global without erasing Indian identity
- The bangle silhouette read in the negative space is a **hidden motif** — discoverable on close inspection, rewards brand-aware customers
- Minimal geometry is infinitely scalable; works identically at favicon and billboard
- Removes the "Indian pattern" expectation, allowing the product photography to do the cultural storytelling

### Typography Recommendation

| Role | Font | Weight | Source |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| **Monogram base (modified)** | [Libre Baskerville](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Libre+Baskerville) | Bold — to be customised | Google Fonts |
| **Wordmark "NAVA CREATIONS"** | [Jost](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Jost) | Light (300), tracked wide | Google Fonts |
| **UI / Body** | [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) | Regular 400 | Google Fonts |

**Typographic treatment**: The monogram NC is a custom glyph built from modified Libre Baskerville letterforms — a designer (not automated) must draw the interlocking. "NAVA CREATIONS" beneath: Jost Light, all-caps, tracking 0.3em — cool, editorial, global-luxury feel.

### Symbol / Icon Concept
An **interlocking N and C** where:
- The N sits full height, standard serif construction, slightly narrowed
- The C is rotated so it **opens to the right**, with its arms extending past the N's vertical strokes on left and right — like a bangle framing the N
- Where the C crosses the N, the intersection is handled with a **stroke break** (the N appears in front of the C), suggesting depth and craft
- The overall outer boundary of the NC monogram reads as a **near-circle** — the implicit bangle
- Stroke weight is consistent; no thick-thin variation (purity of form)
- Final size proportions: approximately a square, 1:1 aspect ratio at the monogram level

### Wordmark Relationship
**Stacked lockup (primary)**: Monogram centred, "NAVA CREATIONS" beneath, all-caps, spaced, in Jost Light
**Horizontal lockup**: Monogram at left, to the right: "NAVA" large in Jost Medium, "CREATIONS" in Jost Light below — two-line right-side text
**Icon-only**: The NC monogram alone — works at all sizes

### Colour Direction
| Colour | HEX | Use |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Warm near-black | `#1A1A2E` | Monogram + "NAVA" wordmark |
| Antique gold | `#C9A96E` | "CREATIONS" sub-text only (decorative use; not for body text) |
| Warm ivory | `#FAF6F0` | Background in primary applications |

**Alternative colourway**: Deep plum `#5C2449` on ivory for a warmer feel. Both should be tested.

### Light / Dark Application
- **On white**: Near-black monogram — sharp, modern, global
- **On ivory `#FAF6F0`**: The warmest and most premium — preferred for brand materials
- **On near-black `#1A1A2E`**: Reversed in warm ivory — dramatic; works for packaging, premium tissue paper
- **On plum**: Gold monogram — most luxurious but also most opulent; reserve for special contexts

### Favicon / Instagram Icon Adaptation
- **32px favicon**: NC monogram, 1px stroke or filled depending on rendering; on ivory background
- **180px**: Clear at this size; no pixelation risk
- **1080×1080 Instagram profile**: NC on ivory square OR white circle; incredibly clean and distinctive in a feed of busy bangle-brand profiles
- **Merchandise/stamp**: The monogram works as a wax seal stamp, embossed packaging seal, hang tag emboss

### Advantages
- Strongly differentiates from every other Indian accessories brand visually (most use florals/motifs)
- Scales perfectly — geometric marks are infinitely crisp
- The hidden bangle motif is a brand story in itself (content, packaging copy)
- International appeal supports future market expansion beyond India
- Works beautifully on premium packaging, embossing, ribbons

### Trade-offs
- Requires a skilled designer to draw the custom monogram — cannot be typeset from an existing font
- Less immediately "Indian" on first glance — may require supporting brand language to establish Indian origin
- Less warm than Direction 1; some traditional customers may find it cold

### Best Fit: Customer Segments
**Strongest resonance with**: Urban contemporary buyers, Gift buyers, Customisation customers, younger bridal customers
**Secondary resonance**: Bulk return-gift buyers (perceived premium elevates the gift)

## Direction 3 — "The Maker's Hand" (Story-Driven Mark)
*Craftsmanship, Indian Origin, Human Process*

---

### Concept
A mark built around a **stylised open hand**, specifically a hand positioned as if offering or receiving — echoing the gesture of *giving* (gifts, return gifts) and *wearing* (bangles placed on wrists by loved ones). Within the palm of the hand, a bangle silhouette is integrated — not placed on top, but woven into the hand's form so they cannot be separated. The mark is hand-drawn in character (but constructed carefully) — with an organic line quality that signals genuine human making. The wordmark beneath uses a warm serif that feels authored, not mechanised.

The concept references the Indian gesture of *hasta mudra* (sacred hand gestures in classical dance and temple iconography) without being religious — the open palm is universal.

### Design Rationale
- In a category where everything is about the product, a human hand says *maker* and *giver* simultaneously
- Directly addresses NAVA's key differentiator: handmade, personalised, craftsmanship
- The bangle-within-hand motif connects the product to the act of giving — powerful for the return-gift and bridal segments
- An organic, human mark stands out against the geometric and floral icons that dominate the category
- Story potential: "The hands that make it, the hands that wear it" — a brand narrative the mark encodes visually

### Typography Recommendation

| Role | Font | Weight | Source |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| **Wordmark "NAVA"** | [Playfair Display](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Playfair+Display) | Regular (not bold) | Google Fonts |
| **Sub-wordmark "CREATIONS"** | [Playfair Display](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Playfair+Display) | Italic | Google Fonts |
| **UI / Body** | [Nunito Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito+Sans) | Regular 400 | Google Fonts |

**Typographic treatment**: "NAVA" in Playfair Display Regular, natural letter-spacing (not wide-tracked), larger than "CREATIONS". "CREATIONS" in Playfair Italic, intimate and personal. The italic feels like a signature — aligning with the maker story.

### Symbol / Icon Concept
An **open palm hand mark** constructed as follows:
- Viewed from the front, slightly angled (3/4 view), four fingers together and thumb slightly apart — not a fist, not a wave
- The hand is stylised to approximately 70% of realistic proportion — reduced fingers, simplified palm
- Inside the palm space (the hollow of the cupped hand), a **perfect circle bangle** is drawn — same stroke weight as the hand outline
- The bangle and hand share one stroke at their intersection — they are drawn as one continuous mark
- Overall style: think between a wood-block print and a refined illustration — intentional human imperfection, not digital perfection
- Stroke: variable-weight, 0.8–1.6pt, with organic line quality (a designer must draw this by hand or with a calligraphic pen tool)
- Scale: the mark fits in a portrait rectangle roughly 3:4 width-to-height

### Wordmark Relationship
**Stacked lockup (primary)**: Hand mark above; "NAVA" below in Playfair Display; "CREATIONS" in italic beneath — the most narrative version
**Horizontal lockup**: Hand mark at left, stacked wordmark to the right — works for website header
**Icon-only**: The hand+bangle mark alone; at small sizes (favicon) the bangle reads clearly; at social sizes the hand is fully legible

### Colour Direction
| Colour | HEX | Use |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Warm terracotta | `#8B4513` | Icon (hand mark) — warmest, most craft-associated |
| Deep plum | `#5C2449` | "NAVA" wordmark |
| Antique gold | `#C9A96E` | The bangle element within the mark |
| Warm ivory | `#FAF6F0` | Background |

**Alternative**: Full plum on ivory — more brand-consistent; terracotta colourway is for craft-forward contexts.

### Light / Dark Application
- **On ivory**: Terracotta hand + plum wordmark + gold bangle — the richest, most complex rendering
- **On white**: Plum hand + plum wordmark — cleaner, works for digital
- **On deep plum**: Ivory hand + gold bangle — reversed, beautiful for packaging
- **On kraft paper**: Plum or black ink — works as a print stamp, matches the craft narrative

### Favicon / Instagram Icon Adaptation
- **32px favicon**: The bangle circle alone (the hand is too complex at 32px) — a plain circle in plum on ivory
- **180px**: Hand + bangle mark; the hand shape is readable at this size
- **1080×1080 Instagram profile**: Hand mark centred on ivory square — deeply distinctive; no other Bengaluru accessories brand has a hand mark
- **Physical stamp**: This direction is the only one that works as a rubber stamp on kraft paper bags — a powerful offline brand touchpoint

### Advantages
- The only direction that encodes the maker narrative directly in the mark
- Highly distinctive — no competitor in the Indian accessories space has a comparable icon
- Tells the gifting story (open hand = giving) without text
- Works beautifully in physical brand touchpoints (stamp, emboss on kraft)
- Strong emotional resonance with the Personally Present brand personality trait

### Trade-offs
- The most execution-sensitive direction — a poor drawing of the hand mark will undermine the brand. Requires a skilled illustrator.
- Cannot be typeset or assembled from components; it must be drawn
- The organic quality may read as "less polished" to customers who equate sleek with premium

### Best Fit: Customer Segments
**Strongest resonance with**: Customisation customers, Bridal customers (gifting narrative), Return-gift buyers
**Secondary resonance**: Craft-aware buyers who value the handmade story

## Decision Checklist for the Business Owner

Before selecting a direction, consider:

- [ ] Which direction best reflects how you want customers to *feel* when they discover NAVA?
- [ ] Is international appeal (Dir 2) or Indian cultural rootedness (Dir 1/3) more important right now?
- [ ] Do you have access to a skilled illustrator who can execute Dir 3? If not, Dir 1 or 2 are safer.
- [ ] Will the logo appear on physical packaging, stamps, or embossed tags? (All three work, but Dir 3 is best for stamps)
- [ ] Should the logo change, or should it evolve the existing visual identity?
