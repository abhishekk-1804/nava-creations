## 2. Summary: Why Concept C Was Disqualified

We believe in complete transparency regarding why Concept C (Artisan Hand) was removed from consideration:
1. **Micro-Scale Collapse:** At 16px and 24px (the size of browser tabs, mobile bookmarks, and small app badges), the hand's five fingers and 8 Kundan stone beads merge into a solid, unidentifiable brownish circle.
2. **Thermal Label Smear:** When printed on single-color 203 DPI parcel shipping labels, the line drawing of the hand cuts directly through the wordmark `"N A V A"`, obscuring your brand name on courier boxes.
3. **Category Lock-In:** A logo depicting a literal hand wearing a bangle restricts your future growth. If you expand into hair accessories, jhumkas, neckpieces, or festive home gifts, the logo becomes visually contradictory.

## 4. Honest Strategic Trade-Off Analysis

| Strategic Dimension | Concept A: Botanical Parijata | Concept B: Modern Monogram |
|---|---|---|
| **Core Brand Persona** | South Indian boutique craft, festive, bridal, warm heirloom | Architectural, cosmopolitan, sleek, contemporary luxury |
| **Color Emotion** | Deep Plum + Antique Gold (Festive, sacred, regal) | Forest Emerald + Champagne Gold (Modern, authoritative) |
| **Strengths** | - Powerful emotional connection for wedding & festive buyers<br>- Exquisite unboxing presence on plum rigid boxes<br>- Distinctive Indian cultural storytelling | - Flawless digital scalability from 16px to giant signage<br>- Highly versatile if expanding into hair, neckwear, or lifestyle<br>- Clean, minimalist international presence |
| **Vulnerabilities** | - Micro-scale (16px) requires dedicated solid-fill icon<br>- Antique Gold cannot be used for body text (must use dark gold)<br>- More traditional aesthetic tone | - Cooler emotional tone; needs rich product photos for warmth<br>- The bangle curve inside the 'C' is subtle and may be missed<br>- Could be perceived as corporate rather than handmade |
