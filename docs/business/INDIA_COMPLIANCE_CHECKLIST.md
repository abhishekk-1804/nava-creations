Engage a CA to determine the correct HSN code and applicable GST rate for each product category.** Always verify current rates at [gstn.gov.in](https://www.gstn.gov.in) and [cbic-gst.gov.in](https://cbic-gst.gov.in).

| Grievance mechanism under DPDPA | Yes | Appoint Data Protection Officer (DPO) or grievance redressal point | High | Business Owner | Pending |
