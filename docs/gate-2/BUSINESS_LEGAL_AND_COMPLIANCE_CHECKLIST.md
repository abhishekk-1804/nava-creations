## 3. GOODS & SERVICES TAX (GST) & INVOICING COMPLIANCE

### 3.1 Harmonized System of Nomenclature (HSN) & Tax Slabs
Under Indian GST classifications for handcrafted jewellery and accessories:
- **Imitation Jewellery & Bangles:** **HSN Code 7117** $\rightarrow$ **3% GST** (or applicable composition rate).
- **Hair Accessories, Pins & Combs:** **HSN Code 9615** $\rightarrow$ **18% GST**.
- **Packaging & Delivery Services:** Included or taxed at applicable service rates.

### 3.2 Tax Invoice Requirements
Every order dispatched by NAVA must be accompanied by a sequentially numbered Tax Invoice containing:
1. Legal Name, Address, and GSTIN of NAVA CREATIONS.
2. Unique, sequential Invoice Number (e.g., `INV-2026-0001`).
3. Date of Invoice generation.
4. Name, Shipping Address, and State Code of the Customer.
5. HSN Code and description of each bangle set.
6. Taxable value, tax rate, and separate columns for CGST + SGST (for Karnataka domestic deliveries) or IGST (for interstate deliveries across India).
7. Digital or physical signature of authorized signatory.

## 4. LEGAL METROLOGY (PACKAGED COMMODITIES) RULES

Every physical jewellery box shipped to a customer must carry a physical label declaring:
- **Generic Name of Commodity:** e.g., "Handmade Glass Bangles / Bridal Chuda Set"
- **Net Quantity:** e.g., "Net Quantity: 1 Set (24 Bangles)"
- **Month & Year of Manufacture:** e.g., "Manufactured: November 2026"
- **Maximum Retail Price (MRP):** Inclusive of all taxes: `MRP ₹3,499.00 (Incl. of all taxes)`
- **Manufacturer / Packer Details:** NAVA CREATIONS, Bengaluru, Karnataka.
- **Consumer Care Details:** Name, address, phone number, and email of person to be contacted in case of consumer complaints.
